02Story and values

“Wróbelek” – the little sparrow from Poznań

Eliza competes for Akademia Judo Poznań. Her nickname, “Wróbelek” (little sparrow), sounds modest – especially once a 182 cm athlete steps onto the mat.

As a cadet she fought at -63 kg, then spent years at -70 kg, where she won three Polish senior titles and medals at international events. In 2025 she moved up to -78 kg.

Off the mat she is a soldier of the Central Military Sports Team (CWZS) and a graduate in Business Law and Management.

Along the way there were injuries and comebacks. In elite sport that is the part of the work people rarely talk about – and the part that teaches you the most.

Judo is a school of respect and discipline

  1. 01

    Respect

    Every bout starts and ends with a bow. Your opponent is a partner who helps you grow.

  2. 02

    Discipline

    Weight, training plan, recovery. Changing weight class takes consistency every single day.

  3. 03

    Comebacks

    Injuries are part of elite sport. What matters is what happens after them.
