04Tax benefit

Sponsorship that also works in your tax return

Under Polish tax law, companies that fund a non-profit sports club, a sports scholarship or a non-mass sports event can use the sponsorship relief and account for 150% of the expense in total.

06FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Will our logo appear on the judogi at competitions?

Yes, in line with the standard sizes for advertisers on judogi: 10 × 10 cm on the arm, 5 × 10 cm on the right side of the chest or 25 × 5 cm on the shoulder straps. The logo can also appear on training wear, tracksuits, the kit bag and on social media.

Can Eliza come to our company?

Yes. Appearances are part of the Main Partner and Partner packages: a talk for your team, a company event or a judo session for employees or their children. Dates are planned around the competition calendar.

What does the agreement look like?

Each agreement is prepared individually. It can be signed with the athlete or through the club – the latter may allow you to use the Polish sponsorship tax relief.

How long does a partnership last?

There are three options: quarterly (Local Partner), annual (Local Partner and Partner) and until 2028, for the whole qualification cycle (all packages, including Main Partner).

Can a small business become a partner?

Yes. The Local Partner package is designed for small and medium-sized businesses from Poznań and Greater Poland.

How is sponsorship treated for tax purposes?

Companies funding a non-profit sports club or a sports scholarship can use the Polish sponsorship relief and account for 150% of the expense in total. See the details above – the form of agreement must be confirmed with your accountant.

What is in the end-of-season report?

The report covers competition results, the national and international events the athlete took part in, and the posts and stories published on social media.
